## 2. Authentic Logo Analysis

* **Shape:** Perfect circular emblem with concentric brand rings.
* **Outer Ring:** Rich Kerala Leaf Green (`#1E824C` / `#176B36`) with clean white uppercase typography: `"IRIS FOOD & KITCHEN PRODUCTS"`.
* **Central Field:** Radiant Warm Mustard/Golden Yellow (`#FFDC33` / `#FFC107`) adorned with a delicate traditional Kerala Kolam/Mandala motif watermark.
* **Character Mascot:** An illustrated cheerful Kerala chef woman in a chef's toque and traditional printed saree with shawl, performing the celebratory "chef's kiss / delicious" gesture.
* **Malayalam Lettering:** Prominent, flowing, authentic Malayalam script: **വീട്ടിലെ അച്ചാർ** in bold charcoal black with high contrast.
* **Certifications & Accreditations:**
  - `"100% NATURAL"` leaf badge in deep green.
  - FSSAI License: `fssai 213252020000459`.
  - English script: `"VEETTILE ACHAAR"` in bold stylized serif/display font.
