# Design Direction — Veettile Achaar

## 1. Creative Concept: "From a Kerala Kitchen to Your Table"
Veettile Achaar is positioned not as an industrial food product, but as an authentic, comforting artisanal luxury — the exact taste of a Kerala grandmother's sun-ripened pickling jar brought to modern dining tables.

The design embodies:
* **The Warmth of Heritage:** Warm teak, clay Bharanis, brass Urulis, sun-dried chilies, and banana leaf green.
* **Modern Editorial Elegance:** Spacious editorial layouts inspired by Kinfolk and Bon Appétit, with generous whitespace, confident serif headlines, and tactile photography.
* **Conversion-Driven E-Commerce:** Clean interactive cards, transparent pricing, pack variant selector, instant sticky cart drawer, bulk enquiry workflow, and one-click WhatsApp dispatch.

---

## 2. Typography Hierarchy
1. **Editorial Display:** Playfair Display / Cormorant Garamond / Instrument Serif style via Google Fonts — for grand headlines that evoke gastronomic craftsmanship and heritage.
2. **Interface Sans:** Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans — for ultra-clean, legible navigation, pricing, button copy, and product descriptors.
3. **Malayalam Typography:** Anek Malayalam / Noto Serif Malayalam — ensuring authentic Malayalam lettering renders with dignity and clarity.

---

## 3. Design Tokens & Visual Hierarchy
* **Backgrounds:**
  - Cream Canvas: `#FAF8F5`
  - Crisp Card White: `#FFFFFF`
  - Dark Heritage Green Accent: `#123824`
  - Subtle Border: `#EAE4DC`
* **Brand Accents:**
  - Deep Spiced Emerald: `#1B5E20`
  - Vibrant Leaf Green: `#2E7D32`
  - Sunlit Mustard Gold: `#D99B00`
  - Warm Kerala Chili: `#C62828`
* **Touch Targets & Spacing:**
  - Minimum 48px comfortable touch targets on mobile.
  - Smooth rounded corners (`rounded-2xl` on cards, `rounded-full` on pills and action buttons).
  - Subtle multi-layered soft drop shadows (`shadow-[0_8px_30px_rgb(0,0,0,0.06)]`).
